# Holistic Admissions Standards & Endowment Fellowship Allocation

## 1. Institutional Governance & Legal Standards
Admit Scholar Admissions Evaluator operationalizes holistic university admissions review in conformity with:
- **Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. § 1001 et seq.)**
- **Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA, 34 CFR Part 99)**
- **U.S. Supreme Court Rulings on Holistic University Admissions**

---

## 2. Holistic Review Index Formulation
The composite evaluation index balances standardized academic trajectory with contextual socioeconomic adversity:

$$\text{Composite Index} = 0.50 \cdot \text{AI}_{\text{norm}} + 0.30 \cdot \text{Curricular Rigor} + 0.20 \cdot \text{Contextual Resilience}$$

Where:
- $\text{AI}_{\text{norm}}$: Standardized Academic Index combining unweighted GPA ($4.0$ scale) and standardized percentile test ranks.
- **Curricular Rigor**: Ratio of Advanced Placement (AP), International Baccalaureate (IB), or dual-enrollment credits taken relative to offerings available at the secondary school.
- **Contextual Resilience**: Multiplier derived from first-generation college status, local school poverty index, and economic hardship overcome.

---

## 3. Endowment Scholarship & Fellowship Allocation Bylaws
Allocations from university endowment fellowship funds adhere to strict criteria:

### A. Presidential Academic Fellowship
- **Criteria**: Composite Index $\ge 95.0$.
- **Award**: $\$25,000.00$ annual tuition grant, renewable for 4 years of undergraduate study.

### B. Opportunity & First-Generation Endowment Grant
- **Criteria**: Demonstrated financial need $\ge \$40,000.00$ annually combined with first-generation college applicant status.
- **Award**: $100\%$ full-tuition coverage plus on-campus housing allowance.

### C. Women & Underrepresented STEM Fellowship
- **Criteria**: Enrolled major in Engineering, Mathematics, or Computer Science with Composite Index $\ge 90.0$.
- **Award**: $\$15,000.00$ annual research stipend and direct faculty mentorship.
